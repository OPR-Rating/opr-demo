Add the app to your phone

On Android (Chrome): open the app link → tap the ⋮ menu → “Add to Home screen”.
On iPhone (Safari): open the app link → tap the Share icon → “Add to Home Screen”.
It then works just like an app, with its own icon — one tap and you're on your club.

What you can do in the app

Enter your match scores with your personal PIN (ask your admin if you don't have it) · follow your rating and form match by match · see how you play with each partner · climb the club ladder · enter the tournaments and follow the draws and standings live. Your scores count the moment they're entered — the ratings update automatically.
