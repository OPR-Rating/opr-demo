Add the app to your phone

📲 Show me how to install it ›
On iPhone (Safari): open Your Club App → tap the Share icon ⬆️ → “Add to Home Screen”.
On Android (Chrome): open Your Club App → tap the ⋮ menu → “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).
It then works just like an app, with its own OPR icon — one tap and you're on your club, no browser bar, no fuss.

What you can do

🎾 Inside the app ›
1Enter your match scores with your personal PIN (ask your admin if you don't have it yet).
2Follow your rating and form match by match as your story builds.
3See how you play with each partner — every pairing rated on its own.
4Climb the club ladder and issue your challenges.
5Enter the tournaments and follow the draws and standings live.
Your scores count the moment they're entered — the ratings update automatically.
